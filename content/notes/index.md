## LaTeX rendering example

Equations render natively. The Thiele equation for vortex core dynamics:

$$\mathbf{G} \times \frac{d\mathbf{X}}{dt} - \hat{D} \cdot \frac{d\mathbf{X}}{dt} + \mathbf{F}(\mathbf{X}) = 0$$

where $\mathbf{G} = G\hat{z}$ is the gyrovector, $\hat{D}$ the damping tensor, and $\mathbf{F}$ the effective force on the vortex core.
